How to Use WanderShare

Everything you need to know - sign in, share trips, save places, follow explorers, use the map and more.

📋 Quick Guide

🚀
Step 01

Getting Started

WanderShare is a family-friendly travel community where real explorers share real stories, photos and adventures from every corner of the globe. Here is the quick tour of the pages:

  • 🏠 Home - the welcome page with the latest adventures and top members.
  • ✨ Inspire Me - get random destination ideas and explore travel vibes.
  • 🌍 Explore - the community feed with the Live Global Travel Map.
  • 🗺️ Countries - browse countries and see how many community stories each has.
  • ✈️ Community - share your own trip with photos and a story.
  • 👤 Profile - your account, saved places, posts and settings.
💡

Everything is saved to the cloud (Firebase), so your posts are visible to everyone on every device. You can also browse as a guest without an account.

🔑
Step 02

Sign In With Google

To post, like, follow, save or comment you need a real Google account. Here's how:

  • Click Profile in the menu (or "Login / Sign Up").
  • Click "Continue with Google" (or "Sign Up with Google" if you're new).
  • Choose your Google account and accept - done! No password needed.
👻

Don't want an account? Click "Browse as Guest" to look around. Guests can view everything but cannot post, like or follow.

✈️
Step 03

Share a Trip

Posting your adventure is easy. You must be signed in with a real account:

  • Go to Community → Share a Trip (or Profile → Share a Trip).
  • Fill in the title, destination, country and region.
  • Add up to 6 photos (JPG, PNG or WebP).
  • Write a short description and your full story (max 2,000 characters).
  • Pick some tags like Beach, Mountains or Food.
  • Choose visibility: 🌍 Public (everyone), 👥 Followers, or 🔒 Only Me.
  • Tick the guidelines box and click "Publish My Trip".
🌍

Public posts are shared instantly with everyone and appear as a ✈️ pin on the Live Global Travel Map.

📸
Step 04

Photos & Content Rules

  • Up to 6 photos per post, each under 5MB. Videos are not supported.
  • Photos are automatically compressed to keep the site fast.
  • English only - posts in other languages are automatically blocked.
  • No duplicates - posting the same story, title or photos is blocked.
  • No offensive or explicit content - bad words and adult links are blocked.
  • Photos must be yours or shared with permission.
  • Right-click saving is disabled - only the photo's owner can download their own pictures.
🌍
Step 05

Explore & the World Map

The Explore page is the heart of the community:

  • 🗺️ Live Global Travel Map - every public post appears as a ✈️ pin. Click a pin to see the photo and read the story.
  • Filter tabs - All, Following, Europe, Asia, Americas, Africa, Oceania and Top Rated.
  • 🔍 Search - type a destination to find trips.
  • Vibe filter - from the Inspire page you can jump straight to a travel style.
🗺️

The map updates live - when someone posts, a new pin appears automatically on every open device.

❤️
Step 06

Like, Follow, Save & Share

Every post has an action bar at the bottom:

  • 🤍 Like - tap the heart to like a post. The count is saved in the cloud, so it stays forever and shows for everyone.
  • 📍 Save - save a place to your profile's personal world map for later.
  • 🔗 Share - share the post with friends (or copy the link).
  • ➕ Follow - follow an explorer to see their trips in the "Following" filter.
⭐
Step 07

Rate & Comment

  • ⭐ Rate - open any post and tap 1-5 stars. You can rate once per place, and your vote is locked after you submit.
  • 💬 Comment - write one comment per place. Must be signed in, English, and no offensive words.
👤
Step 08

Your Profile

  • ✏️ Edit Name - change your display name anytime; it saves forever and updates on all your posts.
  • 🗺️ Saved Map - your saved places and posted trips on a 2D map or 3D globe.
  • 📸 My Posts - see, download or delete your own posts (only you can delete yours).
  • Stats - see your trips, saved places, countries, following and followers counts.
📬
Step 09

Message Box

Every signed-in user has their own message box:

  • Open the 💬 chat assistant (bottom-right corner).
  • Click "📬 Messages" at the bottom of the chat panel.
  • You'll see any messages sent to you - for example, if the admin contacts you about your posts.
🛡️

If the admin deletes your post or bans your account, they must write a reason - and that reason is delivered straight to your message box so you always know why.

💬
Step 10

Chat Assistant

The little 💬 button in the bottom-right corner is the WanderShare assistant. Ask it things like:

  • "Find me a location" - get a random destination idea.
  • "How do I create an account?" - learn about signing in.
  • "Best places on a budget" - budget travel tips.
  • "What should I pack?" - packing suggestions.
  • "What is WanderShare?" - a quick intro to the site.
🚩
Step 11

Report & Safety

  • Open any post and click "🚩 Report Inappropriate Content".
  • Your report goes straight to the admin - the post stays visible until the admin decides.
  • The admin reviews reports and can remove the post or keep it.
  • Never share personal information like phone numbers or addresses in your posts.
⚙️
Step 12

Account & Privacy

  • Sign Out - from the profile Settings card or the user menu in the top-right.
  • Delete Account - removes everything and blocks that email from creating a new account.
  • Your public posts are stored in Firebase and shared with everyone. Private "Only Me" posts stay in your browser.
  • For full details read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
📬

Need help? Contact us at hashenf99@gmail.com or use the report button on any post.
