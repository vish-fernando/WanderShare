Terms of Service

The rules and guidelines that keep WanderShare a safe, friendly and family-friendly travel community.

✅
Section 1

Acceptance of Terms

By creating an account or using WanderShare, you agree to these Terms of Service. If you do not agree, please do not use the site.

👤
Section 2

Your Account

You are responsible for keeping your login details safe and for all activity under your account. You must provide accurate information and keep it up to date.

✈️
Section 3

Your Content

You keep ownership of the photos and stories you post. By posting, you give WanderShare a non-exclusive licence to display your content on the site. You must only post content you own or have permission to share. Public posts are stored in Firebase (Google's secure cloud database) so the community can see them. Private "Only Me" posts stay in your browser and may be lost if your browser data is cleared - deleted content cannot be recovered.

🚫
Section 4

Acceptable Use

WanderShare is family-friendly. Do not post anything hateful, abusive, misleading, unsafe, or that violates someone else's rights. All posts must be written in correct English - non-English content is automatically blocked. Duplicate posts (same story, same title, or same photos) are automatically blocked. Content that breaks these rules may be removed and accounts may be suspended.

🛡️
Section 5

Admin Powers & Moderation

WanderShare is moderated by the site owner to keep the community safe. The admin has the right to:

  • Delete any post that violates these Terms or is reported by the community
  • Ban users who repeatedly break the rules - banned users cannot post
  • Delete all posts by a user who seriously violates the rules
  • Review reports and decide whether to remove or keep a reported post
  • See user emails for security and moderation purposes only (never shown publicly)
  • See user details such as their posts, likes and followers

The admin never shows your email to other users, never reads your private posts, and never sells or shares your personal data. Reports are handled fairly and only rule-breaking content is removed.

⚖️
Section 6

Changes & Termination

We may update these Terms from time to time. Continued use of WanderShare after changes means you accept the updated Terms. We may suspend or remove accounts that violate these rules.

Section 7

Contact Us

Questions about these Terms? Reach us anytime at hashenf99@gmail.com or use the report button on any post.
