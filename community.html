🛡️ Community Guidelines

✅

Family-Friendly Only

All content must be suitable for all ages. No adult or explicit material.

🗣️

Respectful Language

No offensive words, hate speech, or discrimination of any kind.

🇬🇧

English Only

All posts, comments and stories must be written in correct English. Non-English posts are automatically blocked.

📸

Appropriate Photos

Upload only travel photos of places, landscapes, food and culture.

✈️

Travel Content Only

Posts must be about real places you have visited. Keep it genuine!

🤝

Be Kind

Inspire, encourage and celebrate travel. Negativity is not welcome here.

See a post that breaks these rules? Click the 🚩 Report button on any post and we'll review it right away.
