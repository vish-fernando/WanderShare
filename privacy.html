Section 01

Information We Collect

When you use WanderShare, we collect certain information to provide you with the best possible experience. Here's exactly what we gather:

👤

Account Information

Your display name, email address, and profile picture when you register an account.

📸

Content You Create

Travel posts, photos, stories, location data, and tags you share with the community.

📍

Location Data

Country and city names you enter for your posts. We do not collect GPS or device location.

🔧

Usage Data

Basic analytics such as pages visited and interactions, to help us improve the platform.

⚙️
Section 02

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes - and nothing else:

  • To provide the service: Display your posts, manage your account, and enable community features.
  • To verify your identity: Ensure all accounts belong to real people through email verification.
  • To moderate content: Keep the platform safe, family-friendly, and free from spam or inappropriate material.
  • To improve the platform: Analyse anonymised usage patterns to build better features and fix bugs.
  • To communicate with you: Send account-related notifications such as password resets or security alerts.
🔒
Section 03

Image & Photo Protection Policy

At WanderShare, we take photo copyright and creator intellectual property seriously. All photos uploaded by real travellers remain the sole property of their respective creators.

🚫

Anti-Download Protection

Right-clicking images, context menu saving, and drag-and-drop image saving are disabled. Only the photo's original owner can download their own pictures using the "Download" button.

📤

Upload Limits

Users may upload up to 6 photos per travel post to ensure quality and performance. Videos are not supported.

🔍

Content Moderation

All photos are scanned for appropriateness. Explicit, violent, or copyright-infringing material will be removed immediately. All posts must be written in correct English - non-English content is automatically blocked.

©️

Copyright Ownership

You retain full copyright to all content you upload. WanderShare is granted a non-exclusive licence to display your content on the platform only.

⚠️

Storage & Deletion Disclaimer

Public posts are stored in Firebase (Google's secure cloud database) so they can be shared with the community. Private posts marked "Only Me" stay in your browser. If your browser data is cleared, private posts may be lost - they cannot be recovered.

🔑
Section 04

Account Verification & Security

To ensure WanderShare is built by real people sharing genuine travel experiences, all accounts use Google sign-in with email verification.

  • We collect your email address strictly for authentication, account recovery, and security verification.
  • Passwords are handled securely by Google - WanderShare never sees or stores your password.
  • Your account is protected with Firebase Authentication, an industry-standard security service by Google.
  • We will never sell or share your personal contact information with third-party advertisers.
🛡️
Section 05

Admin Powers & Moderation

WanderShare is moderated by the site owner to keep the community safe, family-friendly, and genuine. The admin has the following powers:

🛡️ What the Admin CAN Do

  • View user emails - for security and moderation purposes only, never shown publicly
  • Delete any post that breaks the rules or is reported by the community
  • Ban users - banned users cannot post or participate
  • Delete all posts by a user who violates the rules
  • Review reports - reported posts are sent to the admin, who decides to remove or keep them
  • See user details such as how many posts, likes and followers a user has

🔒 What the Admin NEVER Does

  • Show your email to other users - emails are visible to the admin only
  • Read your private ("Only Me") posts
  • Sell or share your personal data
  • Ban users without a valid reason
  • Change or steal your photos or stories
💡

If you see content that breaks the rules, use the 🚩 Report button on any post. The report goes directly to the admin, who will review it and decide what to do.

🤝
Section 06

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency. Here is our stance on sharing your data:

✅ We DO

  • Use Firebase (Google) for secure authentication and database storage
  • Display your public posts and profile information to other users
  • Comply with legal requirements if ordered by law enforcement

❌ We NEVER

  • Sell your personal data to advertisers or data brokers
  • Share your email address publicly or with other users
  • Track you across other websites or apps
  • Use your photos for advertising without your consent
🍪
Section 07

Cookies & Tracking

WanderShare uses minimal cookies strictly necessary for the platform to function:

  • Authentication cookies: To keep you signed in and remember your session securely.
  • Preference cookies: To remember your settings, such as saved locations and display preferences.
  • No advertising cookies: We do not use any third-party advertising or tracking cookies whatsoever.
💡

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party tracking scripts. Your browsing on WanderShare stays on WanderShare.

Section 09

Children's Privacy

WanderShare is a family-friendly community, however our service is intended for users aged 13 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

⚠️

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will promptly delete it.
